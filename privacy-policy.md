# Privacy Policy for WiFi Seeker

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

## Overview

WiFi Seeker is an Android application designed to scan, analyze, and display information about nearby Wi-Fi networks.

**Your privacy is important to us.** The application processes information locally on your device. The developer does not collect your personal data, has no remote access to information stored by the application, and does not operate any servers or use third-party services to collect user data.

No registration or account is required to use the application.

## Information Access and Processing

To provide its functionality, WiFi Seeker accesses information about nearby Wi-Fi networks, including:

- Network names (SSID) and identifiers (BSSID).
- Signal strength, frequency, security capabilities, and other network characteristics.
- Scan results and related information that may be stored locally for the application's features.

Some of this information may be considered sensitive, as nearby Wi-Fi networks can potentially be used to infer a device's location.

Android requires location permissions to access Wi-Fi scanning results. The application requests these permissions solely to support its scanning functionality, including background scanning when enabled.

All scanned network information is processed locally and is not transmitted to the developer or any data collection service.

## Data Collection and Sharing

**The developer does not collect, receive, or have remote access to your personal data or Wi-Fi scanning results.**

WiFi Seeker:

- Does not require personal information or user accounts.
- Does not upload scanning results or stored network information to remote servers.
- Does not use third-party analytics or tracking services to collect user data.
- Does not sell or otherwise disclose user data to third parties.

The application does not have a server-side component or any associated service intended to receive or store user information.

### User-Initiated Export

The application allows you to export collected information, including in XML format.

Exporting or sharing this information is performed only at your explicit request. You control whether to export the data and where to save or send it.

Once you choose to share exported information with another application, service, or recipient, its subsequent handling is governed by that recipient's privacy practices.

The developer can only receive exported information if you explicitly choose to send it to the developer yourself.

### Internet Access

Certain features may access the internet to retrieve publicly available reference information, such as network equipment manufacturer databases.

Such requests are not used to transmit collected Wi-Fi information or personal data. As with ordinary internet connections, the remote source may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address.

These external sources are not used by the developer to collect or analyze user information.

## Data Storage and Retention

Information generated or collected by the application is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.

You can delete locally stored information using the application's available features or by clearing its data through Android settings. Uninstalling the application also removes its private local data.

Files that you have manually exported or shared are stored separately and must be managed or deleted by you.

### Android Backup

Depending on your device settings and Android version, the operating system may include application data in its backup and restoration mechanisms, including cloud backups.

These operations are managed by Android and your selected backup provider, not by the WiFi Seeker developer.

**The developer has no access to these backups.** Their storage, retention, and deletion are governed by your device's backup settings and the policies of the backup provider.

## Data Security

WiFi Seeker relies on Android's application sandbox and storage security mechanisms to protect locally stored information.

Since the developer does not receive or remotely store your data, there is no developer-operated database or server containing your personal information or scanning history.

The security of exported files, device backups, and information voluntarily shared with other services depends on how those files and services are managed.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
